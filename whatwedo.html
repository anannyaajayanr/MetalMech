WHAT WE DO

What We Do

At Metal Mech, we are passionate about using robotics, engineering, and innovation to solve real-world problems. Through the FIRST LEGO League (FLL), we challenge ourselves to think creatively, work collaboratively, and continuously improve our technical and problem-solving skills while making a positive impact in our community.

Robot Game

Each year, we take on a new global challenge that combines robotics, research, and innovation. We design, build, and program autonomous LEGO robots to complete missions.

Innovation Project

Alongside the robot game, we research a real-world problem, develop an innovative solution, and present our work to a panel of judges.

Learning and Collaboration

Throughout the process, we learn from experts, collaborate with teams from around the world, and apply STEM to create meaningful solutions.

More Than a Competition

For us, FLL is more than a robotics competition. It is an opportunity to grow as engineers, innovators, and leaders while inspiring others, sharing our knowledge, and showing how teamwork and creativity can make a real difference.
