Who We Are

Metal Mech is a team of young innovators and engineers with a passion to learn, create, innovate, and work together.

We are a team of students in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, attending Gems International School. United by our curiosity for science, technology, engineering, and mathematics, we collaborate to design innovative solutions, develop technical skills, and continuously challenge ourselves to grow.

Over the years, we have competed in robotics competitions, developed innovative engineering projects, and earned recognition for our achievements in national and international competitions.

As we continue to grow, we remain dedicated to inspiring others, embracing new challenges, and using engineering to create meaningful solutions that make a positive impact.
