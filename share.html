SHARE

Sharing Our Journey

Throughout the season, we connected with different FLL teams, experts, mentors, and audiences to share our work and learn from others. We met teams from different schools and countries, shared ideas, exchanged experiences, and supported each other through the FLL journey. These interactions helped us understand how other teams approached the robot game, innovation project, teamwork, and problem-solving.

We also spoke with experts who gave us valuable feedback about archaeology, field safety, technology, and how our solution could be improved for real-world use. Their advice helped us make our project more realistic and strengthened the way we explained our problem and solution.

In addition, we presented our work to parents, students, and wider audiences, which helped us improve our confidence, communication, and presentation skills. By sharing our journey with different people, we were able to receive feedback, inspire others, and show that FLL is not only about competing, but also about learning, helping, connecting, and growing as a team.
