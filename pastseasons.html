PAST SEASONS

Past Seasons

Metal Mech has competed in three FIRST LEGO League seasons in total, growing each year through robot design, innovation projects, teamwork, and competition experience. Over time, our team also branched out into more STEM and robotics competitions beyond FLL, allowing us to challenge ourselves in new ways. Through these experiences, we have achieved success regionally, nationally, and internationally, while continuing to learn, improve, and represent our team with pride.
