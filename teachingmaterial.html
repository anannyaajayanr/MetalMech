FLL Guide App

Teaching Beginners in FLL

Metal Mech created an interactive FLL Guide App to help beginner teams understand the robot game in a clear and practical way. It teaches students everything they need to know, from choosing the right gear train, wheels, and robot design choices to planning strategy, improving consistency, and understanding how to approach missions. We have also started testing it with our school’s junior team so we can improve the guide based on real feedback.
